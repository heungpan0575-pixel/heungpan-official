About

Heungpan Commerce Automation is a commerce content automation service designed to help authorized users create, manage, and publish digital content across supported online platforms.

Social platform integrations are used only for accounts explicitly authorized by the account owner.

TikTok Integration

The service may connect to TikTok through official TikTok APIs to upload and manage video content for authorized TikTok accounts. Access is performed only after user authorization.
